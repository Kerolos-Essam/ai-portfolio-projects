Adaptive / Safe GIF export

Preview, WebM and MP4 stay at 60 FPS. GIF uses one worker and encodes one frame at a time.

Safe mode limits clips to 6 seconds / 144 frames. Longer animations export only their beginning. GIF has binary transparency; faint alpha is removed.

On a laptop that has powered off during export, use Safe mode. No setting can guarantee hardware stability. Do not repeat long stress tests.
